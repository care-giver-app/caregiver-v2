Support

Contact us

Email support@caretosher.com. We usually reply within a few days.

To report a bug from the app, use Settings → About this app → Send feedback. It includes the app version and your device, which helps us find the problem.

Close your account

In the app, go to Settings → Close your account and enter your password. This deletes your sign-in and removes your name and email address. Entries you logged stay with your care team, shown as written by a former caregiver.

If you are a care team's only admin, promote someone else first, or close the team if you are the only member.

Delete a care team's data

Closing a care team in the app archives it. To have a team and everything in it permanently deleted, email us from an admin's address and name the team.

Not for emergencies

CareToSher is not a medical service and does not monitor anyone. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.
