Privacy Policy

Effective OPEN: date set when published

CareToSher is an app that lets a family share the care of someone they love. This policy explains what information the app collects, what we do with it, and how to have it deleted. The short version: we use your information only to run the app. We do not sell it, show you ads, or track you.

What we collect

Your account. Your name, your email address, and a password. Passwords are handled by Amazon Cognito and are never stored or seen by us in readable form.

Care information you and your care team enter. This includes the names of the people you care for, trackers and the readings logged in them (which can include health information such as blood pressure, medications and doses), schedules, notes and journal notes, and who logged what and when.

Your care team. Which teams you belong to, your role, and the email addresses of people invited to join.

Feedback. When you send feedback from the app, we receive your message, the app version and your device model. Unless you choose to send it anonymously, it also includes your account so we can reply. We keep feedback for as long as it is useful, with no set end date. You can ask us to delete feedback you sent by emailing support; for anonymous feedback, tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said, since nothing kept with it says it was yours.

Technical logs. Our servers keep request logs, which include your account ID, for up to 30 days so we can find and fix problems.

Information that stays on your phone. If you turn on Face ID, your face data never leaves your device; the app only keeps your sign-in in the iPhone's secure Keychain. Some app preferences are stored on the device and never sent to us.

How we use it

Only to provide the app: to show your care team's information to its members, to keep your account working, to send the reminders and alerts you choose to receive, to answer you when you contact us, and to fix problems. We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it, and the app has no third-party analytics or tracking.

Who can see it

Your care team. Everything entered for a care team is visible to that team's members. Admins decide who is on the team.

Service providers. The app runs on Amazon Web Services, which stores the data on our behalf. Support email is handled by Zoho Mail. They may use the information only to provide those services to us.

When the law requires it. We will disclose information if we are legally required to.

Information about the people you care for

CareToSher holds information about care receivers that caregivers enter, including health information. Please enter it only with the care receiver's permission, or where you are responsible for their care.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Closing your account (Settings → Close your account) deletes your sign-in and removes your name and email address. Entries and notes you wrote stay with your care team, shown as written by a former caregiver, because they are part of the care record the rest of the team relies on.

Care teams closed in the app are archived rather than deleted. To have a team and all of its information permanently deleted, email support@caretosher.com from an admin's address.

You can also email us to ask what information we hold about you or to ask us to correct or delete it.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit and at rest. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach ever affects your information, we will tell you.

Children

Accounts are for adults. CareToSher is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and email you when the change is significant.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@caretosher.com.
